THE NOURISHED GARDEN & FARM
5 Simple Changes for a Calmer Family Week
Nourishing the body from the soil to the soul.

DAY 1 — CREATE ONE ANCHOR
Choose one predictable point in the day: wake-up, after school, dinner, or bedtime. Keep the same three simple steps around that anchor.

DAY 2 — LOWER THE MORNING LOAD
Prepare one thing tonight: clothing, breakfast ingredients, water bottles, or a visual checklist.

DAY 3 — ADD BEFORE YOU REMOVE
Add one nourishing food your family already accepts instead of trying to change the entire menu at once.

DAY 4 — BUILD IN A RESET
Try ten quiet minutes after a busy transition. Dim lights, reduce questions, and offer water or a familiar snack.

DAY 5 — MOVE TOGETHER
Choose ten minutes of family movement: a walk, animal chores, dancing, stretching, or garden work.

DAY 6 — MAKE BEDTIME VISIBLE
Write or draw the bedtime steps where everyone can see them. Keep the order predictable.

DAY 7 — NOTICE WHAT HELPED
Ask: What felt easier? What created stress? Keep one helpful change for next week.

REMEMBER
Small, repeatable changes matter more than a perfect week. Adjust these ideas for your family's needs.
